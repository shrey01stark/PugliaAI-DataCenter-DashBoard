Puglia AI Data Center

€59.61M Renewable-Integrated GPU Infrastructure Platform — Southern Italy
⬤ Recommendation: Conditional Go

Executive Summary

A 6 MW AI compute platform in Puglia, Southern Italy, combining 500-GPU GPUaaS infrastructure with 6.0 MWp on-site solar PV and 5 MW / 10 MWh battery storage. The project targets €16.07M OY3 revenue at a 54.6% EBITDA margin, supported by a project finance structure with €33.37M senior debt (56% LTV), delivering DSCR of 1.88× — materially above the 1.25× lender covenant floor.

The thesis rests on three structural advantages: (1) accelerating European enterprise GPU demand with limited sovereign supply; (2) Puglia solar irradiance (~1,200 kWh/kWp/yr) reducing energy costs to ~7% of OPEX vs. 40–60% industry norm; (3) hybrid revenue model mixing on-demand and reserved-contract tiers to balance growth upside with cash flow predictability.

Risk Register

HIGH Revenue Concentration

44.6% of OY3 revenue (€7.17M) from Stream 4 contracted clients not yet signed at financial close. 30% shortfall alone reduces DSCR to 1.33×.

✓ Mitigant: Pre-close requirement for ≥50% capacity under binding agreement. Sales pipeline must show signed LOIs before close.
HIGH GPU Utilization Ramp

If AI adoption is slower than expected or client acquisition lags, utilization underperforms. -20% utilization stress breaches covenant (DSCR 1.20×).

✓ Mitigant: Pre-sales contracts, flexible Bronze pricing, marketing partnerships with AI tool vendors. DSRA covers covenant breach during ramp.
MEDIUM GPU Pricing Pressure

New GPU chipsets (NVIDIA successor generations, AMD competition) could force price reductions. -15% pricing stress → DSCR 1.43× — manageable.

✓ Mitigant: Long-term Gold reserved contracts with fixed pricing and floors. Price review clauses limited to CPI-linked adjustment only.
MEDIUM Technology Obsolescence

GPUs have 3–5 year lifecycles. Two refresh cycles expected within 10-year debt tenor. Unplanned refresh CapEx would stress CFADS.

✓ Mitigant: €1.4M/year hardware reserve provision. Refresh modelled at Year 4–5 and Year 8–9. Confirmed in financing docs as earmarked reserve.
MEDIUM Construction Risk

CAPEX overrun of +10% increases debt service, reducing DSCR to 1.60×. A 6-month commissioning delay depletes DSRA without any CFADS generation.

✓ Mitigant: Fixed-price EPC contract with LDs for delay. Performance bond. Construction all-risk + delay-in-start-up insurance. Independent Engineer oversight.
MEDIUM Interest Rate Risk

If loan is floating, a 200bps rise increases annual debt service ~€667K, reducing OY3 DSCR from 1.88× to ~1.71× (base). In combined stress, could push below 1.0×.

✓ Mitigant: Fix rate pre-close or purchase interest rate cap/swap. Rate hedge is a condition precedent. Confirmed in term sheet before drawdown.
LOW Solar Underperformance

20% solar output reduction adds only ~€259K energy cost. DSCR remains 1.81×. Energy is already a small fraction of OPEX due to solar integration.

✓ Mitigant: PV performance warranty from EPC contractor. BESS shifts generation to optimize consumption. Weather insurance if required by lenders.
LOW–MED Italian Regulatory Risk

Italy has history of retroactive energy policy changes. Puglia permitting for solar can face regional authority delays. EU grid transition affects interconnection costs.

✓ Mitigant: All key permits in hand before close. Legal opinions on regulatory framework. PPA/energy supply agreement fixes energy cost assumption regardless of policy change.

Conditions Precedent to Funding

These are hard stops. Funding does not proceed until each condition is satisfied.

  • 1
    Binding off-take / capacity reservation ≥50% of GPU capacity
    De-risks the largest revenue assumption (Stream 4). Gives lenders contractual cash flow visibility. Priority: Gold tier first. Must be binding agreements, not LOIs alone.
  • 2
    Fixed-price, date-certain EPC contract with liquidated damages
    Eliminates construction cost and schedule risk from lender exposure. LDs must cover at minimum 6 months of interest carry. Performance bond required from EPC contractor.
  • 3
    Solar / BESS performance warranty or PPA executed
    Locks in the energy cost assumption underpinning the OPEX model. PPA removes price risk; performance warranty addresses yield risk. Either (or both) acceptable to lenders.
  • 4
    DSRA fully funded at €4.0M at financial close
    Ensures OY1–OY2 ramp-period debt service is covered without covenant breach. DSRA = first lien, lender-controlled account. Replenishment is first priority above equity distributions.
  • 5
    All key permits and grid connection approvals in hand
    Eliminates regulatory / permitting risk post-close. Includes Puglia regional authority approvals, Terna grid connection, environmental clearances, and building permits.
  • 6
    Independent Technical Advisor (ITA) report
    Lender-appointed ITA confirms construction plan, ramp schedule, and technical assumptions. Standard in European project finance. Commission at term sheet stage; deliver at close.
  • 7
    Interest rate hedge executed (cap or swap)
    Eliminates floating rate risk from DSCR calculations. Without hedge, 200bps rise costs ~€667K/yr in additional debt service. Cap or fixed-rate swap required before first drawdown.
  • 8
    Full security package documented
    Charges over all project assets, pledge of SPV shares, assignment of all project contracts (EPC, O&M, off-takes, PPA), and full lender control over project bank accounts. Legal opinion required.
  • 9
    Construction all-risk and delay-in-start-up insurance placed
    Standard project finance requirement. DISu insurance covers debt service and fixed costs during a delay caused by insured event. Both policies to be in lender's name / assigned to lenders.
  • 10
    Hardware refresh reserve mechanism confirmed in financing documents
    Lenders' security position must be maintained through GPU lifecycle. Earmark within the €1.4M/yr maintenance reserve for GPU refresh. Model refresh at Year 4–5 and Year 8–9.

Hard Stop: If conditions 1, 2, 4, and 5 are not met before the funding deadline, the recommendation reverts to HOLD. Do not proceed to financial close without these four in place.

Final Recommendation

✓
CONDITIONAL GO
Proceed to financial close — subject to all 10 conditions precedent being satisfied

The project has compelling investment logic: high-margin AI compute infrastructure integrated with low-cost renewable energy in an underserved European market. The financial structure (DSCR 1.88×, 55% EBITDA margin, €4.0M DSRA, 10-year fixed debt) is sound and lender-compatible.

The primary risk is commercial, not technical: Stream 4 contracted revenue (44.6% of OY3 total) must be de-risked through pre-close binding agreements before lenders can underwrite confidently. The combined multi-factor stress test confirms the DSRA is appropriately sized — but it is not a substitute for contracted revenue.

For Lenders

Approve financing only after: binding off-takes for ≥50% capacity; fixed-price EPC with LDs; fully funded DSRA at close; all permits in hand; ITA report; interest rate hedge; and full security package. Covenant testing begins OY2. Minimum covenant: DSCR 1.25× on CFADS. DSRA replenishment is first priority above any equity distribution.

For Equity Investors

Attractive levered IRR of ~18–22% with 2.5× MOIC midpoint and exit value of €60–90M at Year 10. Solar integration creates permanent cost advantage. Expansion optionality (Phase 2 GPU capacity) provides upside not modelled in base case. EU taxonomy alignment opens green bond refinancing in OY4–OY5 at lower cost. Lock-up of ~10 years standard for infrastructure equity.
